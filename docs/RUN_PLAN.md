## Event 1: Port Colborne 100 (100-mile track challenge)
- **Date:** Friday June 13, 2026, 5:30am start
- **Location:** Lakeshore Catholic High School track, Port Colborne ON
- **Format:** 400m track, 402-403 laps = 100 miles
- **Goal:** Finish

### Phases
| Phase | Dates | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Build | Apr 11 – May 9 | Base mileage, fueling practice every 35 min |
| Peak | May 10 – May 30 | 18-22 mi efforts, lock in gear |
| Taper | May 31 – Jun 12 | Drop volume, finalize logistics |
| Race | Jun 13 | 100 miles |
| Recovery | Jun 14-15 | Sleep, eat, walk only |

### Key Runs
- Apr 12: 10 mi (baseline)
- Apr 19: 13 mi
- Apr 26-27: Back-to-back 14 mi / 7 mi
- May 3: 10 mi (recovery week)
- May 10: 18 mi (biggest yet)
- May 14: 6 mi night run with headlamp
- May 17-18: Peak back-to-back 20-22 mi / 10 mi
- May 24: 16 mi confidence run in full race gear
- May 31: 8 mi taper
- Jun 7: 5 mi shakeout
- Jun 11: 2 mi shakeout
- Jun 12: Complete rest

### Fueling Protocol
- Timer every 35 min: gel/real food + 200ml water
- Real food at every hour: banana, PB&J half, boiled potato
- Electrolyte tab every 2 hours
- Practice eating while running

### Race Day Pacing
- Miles 1-40: charging phase — conversational pace only
- Miles 40-70: controlled cruise — do not chase the mile 45 second wind
- Miles 70-90: walk/run 4:1
- Miles 90-100: one more training run

### Mandatory Pit Stops
- Mile 25: crew check-in, IG story, drink
- Mile 40: change socks, reapply Body Glide, 5 min sit max, real food
- Mile 50: halfway — headlamp on if dark, crew lap, IG story
- Mile 70: change socks, second shoes if swollen, IG story
- Mile 100: finish, film everything

### Crew Roles
- **Lap captain:** tracks every lap on whiteboard — does not leave this job
- **Food captain:** monitors 35-min timer, hands food and water, tracks intake
- **Pacer rotation:** friends in 10-15 mile shifts
- **Content crew:** films, posts stories (Muteeb or trusted person)
- **Night crew:** someone committed to staying awake if effort extends past midnight

### Low Patch Protocol (expected miles 55-75)
- Crew instruction: "make me walk one more lap before I quit"
- Action: eat, drink, change socks, sit 5 min max, change audio/stimulation
- Low patches pass in 20-40 min — normal and temporary

### Event Prep Checklist (final 2 weeks)
- [ ] Track booked at Lakeshore Catholic — confirm overnight access
- [ ] Crew schedule confirmed
- [ ] All food prepped and portioned into mile-marker bags
- [ ] Base camp gear packed: canopy, table, chairs, cooler, whiteboard, lights, extension cord, speaker, power banks
- [ ] Body Glide, blister kit, spare socks (3x), backup shoes ready
- [ ] IG content scheduled: announcement reel, countdown posts, race day stories
- [ ] Hashtag locked: #PC100
- [ ] Muteeb briefed on race day content handoff

## Event 2: Niagara Falls International Marathon
- **Date:** Sunday October 25, 2026
- **Goal:** Sub-4:00 (9:09/mile / 5:41/km)

### Phases
| Phase | Dates | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Base Rebuild | Jun 16 – Jul 13 | Rebuild leg turnover post-ultra, all runs by feel |
| Build + Pace | Jul 14 – Aug 17 | Introduce tempo/MP work, long runs to 18 mi |
| Peak | Aug 18 – Sep 21 | Peak long runs 20-22 mi, consistent MP work |
| Taper | Sep 22 – Oct 24 | Drop volume, maintain sharpness |
| Race | Oct 25 | 26.2 mi |

### Key Long Runs
- Jun 21: 10 mi (first post-ultra)
- Jun 28: 11 mi → Jul 5: 13 mi → Jul 12: 14 mi
- Jul 19: 15 mi → Jul 26: 16 mi
- Aug 2: 18 mi (first 18-miler)
- Aug 23: 18 mi → Aug 30: 20 mi (first 20-miler)
- Sep 13: 22 mi (peak — 20 OK too)
- Taper: 16 → 12 → 10 → 8 mi

### Key Tempo/MP Workouts (Wednesdays)
- Jul 15: 4 easy + 2 MP + 1 cooldown
- Jul 22: 4 easy + 3 MP + 1 cooldown
- Jul 29: 3 easy + 4x1mi tempo (8:30-8:45) w/ 90s rest
- Aug 5: 3 easy + 5 MP + 1 cooldown
- Aug 19: 3 easy + 6 MP + 1 cooldown
- Aug 26: 3 easy + 3x2mi tempo (8:30) w/ 2 min rest
- Sep 2: 16 mi long, miles 10-14 at MP
- Sep 9: 3 easy + 8 MP + 1 cooldown
- Sep 16: 20 mi long, last 4 at MP if strong

### Race Day Pacing
- Miles 1-6: 9:20-9:30/mi — start conservative
- Miles 7-18: 9:05-9:15/mi — lock into goal pace
- Miles 19-22: hold form, shorten stride if tired
- Miles 23-26.2: whatever you have left

### Common Sub-4 Mistakes to Avoid
- Going out at 8:50 because you feel great at mile 3
- Skipping fuel because you "don't feel hungry" — eat from mile 6
- Walking through aid stations too long — sip and go
